# Prism Relay language

**Spectrum**: A card's named suit, represented by a color and a geometric mark. _Avoid_: color alone.

**Relay**: The declaration that a player is about to hold one card. _Avoid_: Uno.

**Seat**: One player position: You, Mira or Sol.

**Round**: A deal ending when a seat empties its hand or the table is blocked.

**Match**: Successive rounds until a seat reaches 250 points.

**Active spectrum**: The spectrum required for a spectrum match, including a wild's selected spectrum.

**Drawn choice**: The opportunity to play only a newly drawn card or pass.
